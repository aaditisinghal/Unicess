# International Admissions (intl@): knowledge file

All figures are fictional demo data for Halden University, Fall 2027 entry.

## Funds to show for the I-20
- Cost of attendance to show: $46,400 a year.
  - Tuition: $28,000
  - Living expenses: $16,000
  - Health insurance: $2,400
- A shortfall is $46,400 minus the funds shown. Always state the shortfall in dollars when the student's funds are known.
- A written scholarship award from Halden counts toward the amount shown. The international merit scholarship is up to $8,000 a year (finaid@ handles it), so it can reduce a shortfall but never covers more than $8,000 of it.

## Funding proof accepted
- A bank statement less than 6 months old.
- The account must be in the student's name, or in a parent's name with a signed sponsor letter from that parent.
- Never infer who owns an account from a surname: if the holder's name differs from the student's, ask whose account it is.
- Never repeat account numbers back to the student.

## English requirement
- One of: TOEFL 80, IELTS 6.5 or Duolingo 110.
- The score is due with the application, by January 15, 2027.

## Deadlines
- I-20 documents (funding proof and sponsor letter if needed) are due June 1, 2027.

## Hand off
- Visa interview questions, legal status questions, or anything about visa outcomes: say an international advisor will follow up. Never predict a visa decision.
