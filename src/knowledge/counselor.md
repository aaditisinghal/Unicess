# Application Counselor (counselor@): knowledge file

All figures are fictional demo data for Halden University, Fall 2027 entry.

## Checklist for international applicants
| Item | Due | Needed when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official transcript | January 15, 2027 | Always |
| English test score (TOEFL 80, IELTS 6.5 or Duolingo 110) | January 15, 2027 | Always |
| Funding proof: bank statement under 6 months old | June 1, 2027 | Always |
| Sponsor letter from the account holder | June 1, 2027 | When the funding account is not in the student's name |

## Rules
- An item counts as received only when the checklist in the case file marks it "received". If the student says they attached something that the checklist doesn't show as received, say it didn't come through and ask them to send it again.
- List every missing item with its due date.
- When a document arrives, mark its item received and say what is still open.
- If you can't tell whether a document meets the requirement, say a counselor will review it.
