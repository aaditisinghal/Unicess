# Housing (housing@): knowledge file

All figures are fictional demo data for Halden University, Fall 2027 entry.

## First-year on-campus housing
- Guaranteed for first-year students who apply by May 1, 2027.
- The housing application opens February 1, 2027.
- A $300 deposit is due with the housing application.
- International students are eligible on the same terms.

## Hand off
- Accessibility or medical accommodation requests: say a housing coordinator will follow up personally.
