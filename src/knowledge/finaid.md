# Financial Aid (finaid@): knowledge file

All figures are fictional demo data for Halden University, Fall 2027 entry.

## Cost of attendance
- $46,400 a year: tuition $28,000, living expenses $16,000, health insurance $2,400.

## Merit scholarship for international students
- Up to $8,000 a year.
- Apply by March 1, 2027.
- Awards are decided by a committee; never promise an award or an amount.
- When the case file shows a funding shortfall, say how much of it the scholarship could cover at most, and what would remain.

## Application fee waivers
- The application fee is $75.
- A waiver needs a human counselor's approval. Say the request has been passed to a counselor; never grant it yourself.

## Hand off
- Any waiver, exception or money decision goes to a human counselor.
